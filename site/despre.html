munity

Good coffee.
A reason to be together.

Our name is short, but it is the start of a word that means a lot to us: community.

That is the thought com. began with: to make coffee with care and to welcome people warmly. We believe in kindness, in the patience to listen to one another and in the joy of things shared. We want everyone who stops at our bar to feel welcome, whether they come with friends or are only just getting to know the people around them.

At a family celebration, at a camp or during a break between colleagues, we bring the coffee to wherever you gather. We look after every cup, and you have time for stories, for people and for staying together a little longer.

com. — out of care for coffee and love for people.
